## Navigation and roles

The application navigation is organized by role. Your account can have more than one role, so you may see Agent, Supervisor, and Admin Workspace sections at the same time.

## Agent availability

If you handle interactions, confirm your agent status and queue or campaign activation before beginning work. Availability controls whether new work can be offered to you.

## Get help without leaving your task

Choose **Help** in the application header or press **F1**. If a supported form field is focused, the panel opens its field explanation. Otherwise, it opens help for the current screen.

Use **Open full article** to continue reading here in a reusable documentation tab.

## Prepare your profile and devices

Set [Profile and preferences](/help/profile), including interface and speech language. Connect a supported companion app with [Mobile apps and devices](/help/mobile). Choose the device for calls before becoming Available; microphone selection alone does not select it.

For additional interaction modes, read [Video calls and Vision](/help/agent/video-calls), [Co-browsing](/help/agent/cobrowsing) and [Devices and call handoff](/help/webrtc-phone/devices). Your permissions and the channel configuration determine which controls appear.
