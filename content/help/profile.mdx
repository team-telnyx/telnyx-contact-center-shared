Open **Profile** from your account menu. The page has **Profile**, **Security** and **Devices** tabs. Account preferences do not grant additional screens or operations; an administrator manages your roles.

## Language and personal details

In **Profile**, edit the permitted personal details and picture, then save. **Interface language** changes the application's interface for your account across devices. **Agent speech language** is separate and is used by speech-related features such as live translation. Changing either preference does not translate customer messages or saved content automatically.

If the interface-language update fails, retry after resolving the error; do not assume the displayed draft was saved. Help articles are maintained in English even when the surrounding interface uses another language.

## Notification sounds

The notification controls show your effective channel sounds. Preview a tone and adjust the available channel, volume and loop controls, then use **Save notifications**. These preferences have their own save action. Restoring system defaults removes your personal overrides so later system changes apply.

Keep Agent Desktop open for browser interaction sounds. Browser autoplay restrictions, tab muting and system volume can silence a saved tone. Use **Enable notification sounds** when prompted. Sound preferences do not activate a channel or change routing eligibility.

## Security

Use **Security** for the available password controls. Follow the validation shown on the page and wait for confirmation before signing out. A third-party sign-in and a local password are separate authentication methods; the page shows the controls available to your account.

## Devices

The Devices tab contains **Call devices** and **Mobile app** setup. Review which device takes calls, forget a retired phone, or copy the setup link for a supported companion app. Follow [Mobile apps and devices](/help/mobile) and [Devices and call handoff](/help/webrtc-phone/devices).

The legacy Agent Configuration page only displays a placeholder. Use Profile for the preferences described here.
