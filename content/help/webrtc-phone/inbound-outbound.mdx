## Inbound

When the phone is ringing, **From** shows the incoming caller and the panel exposes **Reject** and **Answer**. Answer within the queue timeout. Reject ends the local offer; for a queued agent call it is recorded as a pre-answer no-answer signal and can affect agent status/routing.

Do not answer a call whose Desktop interaction has already disappeared after timeout/re-enqueue. Wait for the current state to settle or refresh the authoritative interaction list.

## Outbound

Select the Contacts button or the read-only **To** field to open number selection. Choose a phone number or supported SIP URI. **From** is read-only and comes from the profile/call context. Select **Call**; while the attempt is dialing or ringing, **Disconnect** cancels it.

The call button remains disabled until prerequisites such as a destination and ready client are satisfied. Verify country code/E.164 formatting and the intended contact field before placing the call.
