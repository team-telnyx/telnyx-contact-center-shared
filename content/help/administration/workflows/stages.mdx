Stages group related work and define the main conversation order. Put prerequisites before dependent questions, and keep each stage focused on one outcome such as identification, discovery, resolution, or confirmation.

Within a stage, order items as the agent should normally address them. The earliest open item becomes important context for matching ambiguous customer answers. Avoid placing similarly worded slots far apart without clear labels and hints.

A good stage has a clear name, concise agent-facing purpose, required items that can actually be completed at that point, and optional items only for genuinely optional information. Use a final confirmation/read-back item after the slots it summarizes.
