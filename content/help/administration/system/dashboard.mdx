Open **Admin → System → Dashboard**. Review the configuration inventory, integration status and platform activity cards, then follow the relevant module to inspect or change its configuration.

## Interpret the overview

Wait for loading to finish before interpreting counts. A failed request is different from an empty inventory. Refresh after a configuration change and investigate an error in the relevant module rather than assuming the resource has been deleted.

Inventory and connectivity indicators are starting points for diagnosis. They do not establish that a phone call, message, AI response or video session works end to end. Use a controlled test of the affected channel and inspect its interaction history.

## Find the relevant settings

Use [System Settings](/help/administration/system/settings) for operational policies, [AI Copilot](/help/administration/system/ai-copilot) for AI defaults, and [Schedules & calendars](/help/administration/system/schedules) for routing hours. For a failed live operation, correlate its time and interaction ID in [Logging](/help/administration/logging).
