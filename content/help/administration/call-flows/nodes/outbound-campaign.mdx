---
title: Outbound Campaign
description: Start a flow for an outbound campaign contact.
---

**Outbound Campaign** starts an outbound execution and exposes campaign/contact data, including the configured contact alias such as `contact_data` or `contact_record`.

Map the destination and personalization fields before Dial. Add invalid-number, no-answer, busy, rejected, and retry-limit handling. Test only with approved destinations and conservative concurrency/volume limits.
