---
title: Dial Number
description: Place an outbound call leg to a number or expression result.
---

**Dial Number** calls a configured destination, which may contain a variable/expression. Configure caller identity and call options exposed by the editor.

Handle answered, busy, no-answer, rejected, timeout, and error results separately where available. Normalize numbers to E.164, avoid dialing an empty expression, and never route automated tests to unrestricted destinations.
