Open **Admin → Automations → Forms**. Forms collect structured information in the agent experience and can execute Call & App Flow data actions.

The gallery shows name, slug, status, category, assigned queues, preview, and actions. Use **New form**, **Import JSON**, edit, publish, export, archive, and **Show Archived**. Imports are drafts and must be reviewed before publication.

The editor rail contains **AI**, **Pages**, **Blocks**, **Data Actions**, **Media**, and **Templates**. Form metadata includes name, unique slug, description, category, status, queues, auto-open behavior, and theme.
