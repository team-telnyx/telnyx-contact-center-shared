**AI** generates or revises form structure from instructions. Describe users, purpose, pages, required fields, choices, actions, and constraints. Treat the result as a draft: verify IDs, variables, required rules, accessibility, and actions.

**Media** selects reusable images. Confirm the URL remains available to agents, dimensions fit the container, scaling is acceptable, and alt text communicates meaning.

**Templates** seed a reusable structure. After applying one, replace sample labels/options/defaults, review queue assignments, and reconnect environment-specific data actions and media.
