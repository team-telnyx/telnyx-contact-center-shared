Pages define top-level form steps. Each page has an ID, title, description, icon, and ordered root blocks. Add pages for meaningful stages, not every small group of fields.

Use unique stable page IDs. Reorder pages and blocks to match agent work. Nested blocks belong to their container and should not also be assigned as page roots. Preview navigation, keyboard focus, required fields, and what happens when an agent moves backward after entering data.
