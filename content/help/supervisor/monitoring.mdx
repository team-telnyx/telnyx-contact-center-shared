Open **SUPERVISOR → Monitoring**. The selected rail section is preserved and can also be addressed with the `section` URL parameter.

## Overview and Dashboard

**Overview** is the real-time contact-center performance dashboard. **Dashboard** focuses on today's calls and live signals, including Total calls, Answered, Abandoned, Average wait, average handle time, and hourly answered/abandoned volume.

## Agents

The Agents section supports name, status, and queue filtering. Summary cards show roster coverage, available agents, live conversations/occupancy, active queue assignments, and completed calls. Each agent row can expose:

- current configured status and status-changing action;
- activated queue assignments and campaign activations;
- skills and proficiency on hover;
- active calls and the **Supervise this call** action.

Changing another agent's status is an operational intervention. Confirm the agent is not handling or wrapping a call and record the reason according to local policy.

## Queues

Queue metrics include monitored queues, waiting callers, longest wait, active calls, available/busy agents, answered volume, and service level. Required-skill indicators show original or relaxed proficiency requirements; a downward marker indicates relaxation has been applied.

Use filters to isolate pressure before intervening. A high waiting count with available agents can indicate skill mismatch, inactive agent queue assignments, status/endpoint issues, or routing constraints rather than insufficient headcount.

## Previewing live email

Choose **Preview conversation** on an email interaction. Email cards fill the conversation column with compact internal spacing, while the modal retains its standard size. Its format and image settings follow **Admin → Email → Preview**.

During an active interaction, the current agent's saved reply appears with a **Draft** badge, the agent's name and the last saved time. Typing and replies inserted from AI Copilot appear after the agent's automatic save (normally after a brief typing pause), followed by the live stream update. This is a read-only view; an unsaved local edit is not yet visible.

Once the agent sends, the draft is replaced by the outbound message. **Sent** means Telnyx accepted the send; recipient delivery evidence is displayed separately. Scheduled, pending and failed sends retain their respective statuses. Empty drafts disappear, and drafts from previous assignees or closed interactions are not shown. Draft attachments are listed by name, without exposing their unsent contents or BCC recipients.

## Supervising a live call

Open the supervisory console from an active call and choose the supported supervision role, such as monitor, whisper, or barge. The target call must still be active. Announce or record supervisory participation where law and organizational policy require it.

## Channels, availability and devices

Use channel and queue filters to separate voice/video pressure from messaging workload. Agent presence is global, while offers and active work have channel-specific capacity. A user can be visible and Available but have no reachable selected voice device. Review Calls on/device state and queue activation before changing routing.

A device handoff preserves the interaction while changing the handling device. Do not count that as a second customer call. Pending offers, active work and wrap-up occupy capacity; a different browser tab does not add another agent.

## Video supervision

Open **Supervise video call** on an active video interaction. The available modes depend on permissions. Monitor observes/listens, Whisper addresses the agent, and Barge joins the conference. The supervisor's camera control is available in Barge. Switching mode changes who receives the supervisor's media; verify the displayed mode before speaking.

The agent sees a supervision badge. Ending supervision leaves the customer's interaction active. Camera permission, room readiness and the target interaction's active state still apply. See [Video calls](/help/agent/video-calls) for participant controls and Vision consent, which is separate from human supervision.
