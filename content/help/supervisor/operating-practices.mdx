## During operations

1. Start with Monitoring Dashboard for service pressure.
2. Filter Agents and Queues to locate the cause before changing status, routing, or skill requirements.
3. Use live supervision only on an active call and under recording/consent policy.
4. Record the reason for material operational intervention outside the dashboard if your process requires an audit trail.

## Review and coaching

Use Analytics to identify a repeatable pattern, Call History to inspect representative interactions, and Quality to score against a published rubric. Do not coach from one aggregate metric or one AI-generated result alone.

Check data completeness: wrap-up coverage, recording/transcript availability, comparable queues/shifts, and the selected timezone/date range. Separate routing problems, staffing constraints, process gaps, and individual performance before assigning corrective action.
