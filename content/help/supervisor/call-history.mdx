Call History is the last section of the Analytics rail. Filter the list by the available date, direction, queue, agent, state, number, or search controls before opening an interaction.

The detail page contains interaction identity and operational timestamps plus these tabs:

| Tab | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Timeline | Ordered IVR, queue, alert, interaction, hold, transfer, recording, wrap-up, and ended segments. |
| Recording | Playback of an available call recording. Availability depends on recording configuration and retention. |
| Transcript | Speaker-attributed transcription captured for the interaction. Treat text as an imperfect recognition result. |
| AI Assistant | AI-related conversation information when the call used an assistant. |

Workflow history can show stages, Action/Question/Topic/Data items, completion, and captured slot values. Compare the transcript source utterance and confidence before treating an extracted value as verified.

Missing recording, transcript, or AI tab data can be expected when the relevant service was not enabled, failed to start, or data was removed by retention. Do not infer that the interaction itself did not occur.
