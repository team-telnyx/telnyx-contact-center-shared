Supervisor Workspace turns live and historical interaction data into operational views. The main application menu exposes **Monitoring**, **Analytics**, and **Quality** to supervisor/admin/owner roles. **Outbound Dialer** is currently restricted to owner access.

| Workspace | Primary purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Monitoring | Observe current service conditions, agents, queues, waiting callers, and active calls. |
| Analytics | Analyze queue, agent, abandonment, adherence, transfer/hold, wrap-up, AI handoff, outbound, skills, and call-history data. |
| Quality | Review score trends, evaluate recorded conversations, and manage quality scorecards. |
| Outbound Dialer | Configure and operate campaigns, contact eligibility, compliance controls, and live outbound calls. |

Use Monitoring for immediate intervention and Analytics for trend-based decisions. Apply the same date range and filters before comparing reports. An empty result can mean no data in the selected scope rather than a platform failure.
