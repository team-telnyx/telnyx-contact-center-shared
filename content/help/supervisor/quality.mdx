Open **SUPERVISOR → Quality**. Select **1 day**, **7 days**, **30 days**, or a custom date range for Dashboard and Evaluations. Forms are definition data and do not use the reporting range.

## Dashboard

Review average scores, evaluation activity, AI versus human evaluation mix, and trends across agents, queues, and forms. Ensure the selected forms and populations are comparable before ranking agents.

## Evaluations

Filter conversations and select a quality form. Evaluation states include Not evaluated, Draft, AI processing, AI draft, Reviewed, Final, and Disputed. Open a conversation to listen to the recording, inspect transcript/context, and score criteria manually or initiate the supported AI draft.

An AI draft is not final. Review evidence, criterion comments, critical failures, weighting, and total score. Move to Final only after required human review; use Disputed according to the organization's appeal process.

## Forms

Quality Forms are scorecards made of sections and criteria. Provide a form name, at least one section and criterion, clear criterion labels, weights/scoring rules, and AI rubric text where automated assistance is expected. Save as draft while designing, Publish for operational use, and Archive instead of deleting a form referenced by evaluations.
