ℹ️ How It Works

This tool connects directly to your Telnyx AI Assistant using @telnyx/ai-agent-lib in unauthenticated mode. It establishes a real WebRTC voice call, then injects text responses via sendConversationMessage(). The AI processes these as if spoken by a caller.

Requirements: Your AI Assistant must have "Support Unauthenticated Web Calls" enabled.
