🔬 How Audio Injection Works

  1. Mock getUserMedia - We override the browser's microphone API to return our controlled stream
  2. AudioContext - Create an audio context with a MediaStreamDestination
  3. TTS Generation - When we need to "speak", we call Telnyx TTS API to generate audio
  4. Audio Injection - The TTS audio is decoded and played into our mock microphone stream
  5. WebRTC Sends - The Telnyx library sends our injected audio to the AI assistant
  6. AI Responds - AI hears real audio and responds with voice
